01 · THE POOL

The stock is the currency

Stock-paired launchpads let a token go live with a Robinhood Stock Token as its quote asset instead of ETH or a stablecoin. So the market isn't $RIVN/ETH — it's $RIVN against tokenized RIVN. Every buy and sell in this coin is a trade in the stock token too.

02 · THE FLOAT

Buying pulls float into the pool

Stock tokens handed over on a buy stay in the pool as liquidity. Enough of them, and a single memecoin ends up holding a serious chunk of a ticker's on-chain float — BONER got to roughly 53% of the tokenized HIMS supply, leaving thin inventory everywhere else.

03 · THE CEILING

Nobody can mint their way out

Only an authorised participant issues new stock tokens; traders can't. With the exchange shut, on-chain HIMS printed $132.64 against a $28.84 Friday close — about 4.6x — until roughly 4,000 fresh tokens were minted on Monday and it normalised.
